Independent digital art studio

Artwork that gives your screen its own atmosphere.

Wallora is a freelance project creating high-quality digital artwork and wallpapers. Each design is created by the artist behind Wallora, with its own subject, mood, color palette, and visual character.

Made by the creator

Wallora is a creator-led freelance project. Every wallpaper begins with an original creative direction and is developed into a finished piece of digital artwork. The creator shapes the scene, composition, color, and details instead of relying on a one-size-fits-all template.

The goal is to make artwork that feels considered and memorable, whether it is a quiet landscape, a vivid city at dusk, or a scene inspired by cultural heritage. Each design is intended to stand on its own and bring a distinct feeling to the screen.

Custom, distinctive collections

Every collection has its own visual identity. Some lean into calm water, open skies, and gentle light. Others bring together dramatic architecture, glowing city scenes, mountain silhouettes, or the contrast of a bold sunset against a quiet horizon.

From Samurai’s Horizon and Sunset Reflections to the heritage and landscape designs, the collection is built around variety. The artwork is made to offer more than a background: it can set a tone for a workspace, add personality to a phone, or make a familiar screen feel new again.

Designed for everyday screens

Wallpapers are digital artwork, created to bring a strong focal image and a pleasing sense of balance to your device. The collections include Wide Monitor, Mac, and iPhone format tags so you can explore options suited to different screens.

Whether you prefer a spacious landscape, a softly colored sunset, or a richly detailed scene, Wallora is made for people who enjoy adding art and atmosphere to the devices they use every day.

A small independent freelance project

Wallora is built and cared for by its creator as an independent freelance project. Each new design is an opportunity to experiment with a different place, light, palette, and point of view, while keeping the artwork personal and carefully finished.

Thank you for taking the time to explore the work and support independent digital art.
